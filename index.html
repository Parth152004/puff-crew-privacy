Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026. This policy describes Puff Crew: Rapids, published under the developer name Ramo Labs, and related support requests. Contact: jayvekaria282@gmail.com.

100% Free Game — No In-App Purchases or Ads

Puff Crew: Rapids is 100% free to play. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, paywalls, or advertisements. All river courses, game modes, and expedition outfits are completely unlocked and free for every player.

The game on your device

You do not need a game account. Ramo Labs does not collect or run analytics, advertising SDKs, location tracking, public matchmaking or hosted gameplay servers. Settings, best times, and selected outfits are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Nearby local multiplayer

When you host or look for a crew, the app uses your local Wi-Fi network. Discovery broadcasts communicate crew availability, course, room code, and player count directly between devices. Devices on the same Wi-Fi can see the host's local IP address. A crew connection exchanges controls, outfit choices, and shared game state directly between the host and crewmates. Ramo Labs does not receive or log those local packets on a company server.

Support messages

If you email us for support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you provide. We use support messages only to answer questions, resolve technical issues, and improve the game. We do not sell support messages or use them for marketing.

Retention, deletion and choices

Local game data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall the game. You may request deletion of any support email correspondence by contacting jayvekaria282@gmail.com.

Audience and security

This release is designed for teens and adults aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through the game. If you have questions about privacy, email jayvekaria282@gmail.com.
