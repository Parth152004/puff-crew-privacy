RAMO LABS · PUFF CREW: RAPIDS

Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026. This policy describes Puff Crew: Rapids, published under the developer name Ramo Labs, and related support requests. Contact: jayvekaria282@gmail.com.

Free game with ads, no in-app purchases

Puff Crew: Rapids is free to play. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paywalls. All river levels, game modes, outfits and raft flags are free; extra levels and cosmetics unlock only by playing. From version 2.1.0 the game shows ads to support development (see below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. A full-screen ad may appear at a natural break: after you finish a solo river and choose what to do next. Ads never appear while you are sailing, never in the first few rivers you play, and never during local Wi-Fi crew play. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID;
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address;
  • information about your interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Your choices in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time: open the game's Settings and tap Ad privacy choices.

The game on your device

You do not need a game account. Apart from the Google AdMob software described above, Ramo Labs does not run analytics, location tracking, public matchmaking or hosted gameplay servers. Settings, level progress, stars, collected shells, best times, play statistics, badges, best-run replays (ghosts) and selected outfits and flags are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are never sent to us. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Nearby local multiplayer

When you host or look for a crew, the app uses your local Wi-Fi network. Discovery broadcasts communicate crew availability, the selected level, room code, player count and the game version directly between devices, so phones running different versions can tell you to update. Devices on the same Wi-Fi can see the host's local IP address. A crew connection exchanges the game version, controls, outfit and flag choices, and shared game state directly between the host and crewmates. Ramo Labs does not receive or log those local packets on a company server.

Support messages

If you email us for support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you provide. We use support messages only to answer questions, resolve technical issues, and improve the game. We do not sell support messages or use them for marketing.

Retention, deletion and choices

Local game data, including progress and replays, remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. Data processed by Google for ads is kept and protected according to Google's policies. You may request deletion of any support email correspondence by contacting jayvekaria282@gmail.com.

Audience and security

This game is designed for teens and adults aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through the game. If you have questions about privacy, email jayvekaria282@gmail.com.
